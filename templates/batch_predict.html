Upload a class CSV

Required columns: studytime (1-4), failures (0-10), absences (0-100) and G1 (0-20). Optional: student_name, G2 (0-20), and G3, the actual final grade. With G2 the full-term model is used; without it, the early-warning model. With G3 the system also scores its own accuracy. Comma- and semicolon-separated files both work.
