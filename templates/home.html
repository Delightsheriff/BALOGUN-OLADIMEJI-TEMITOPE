Students' Academic Performance Prediction System

Using the Decision Tree Algorithm

This system predicts whether a student is likely to finish a course in the Low, Medium or High performance band, using five records a lecturer already has: study time, past failures, absences, and the first and second period grades. Every prediction shows the exact rules the Decision Tree followed, so an adviser can see why a student was flagged.

How it works

1

Enter student data

Type in one student's records, or upload a CSV for a whole class.

2

The Decision Tree classifies

A trained CART model passes the record through a small set of learned if-then rules.

3

Read an explained result

You get Low, Medium or High, the model's confidence, and the rules that led there.

What the categories mean

Low

Expected final grade below 10/20. The student is at risk of failing.

Medium

Expected final grade from 10 to 14/20. A pass.

High

Expected final grade of 15/20 or above.
